Experience — five more drafts

A second round of five drafts, all built from the same three roles. Cut the ones you don’t want; the one you keep goes into the home page, between Projects and About.

01

The tape measure

Your 21 months drawn to scale: one tick a month, each role as long as it lasted. Drag the hook, or use the arrow keys, and the role under it reads out. It shows at a glance that the career runs without gaps.

02

Lanyard badges

One staff badge per role, hanging from a rail. Press a badge and it turns over to show what the role produced.

03

The passport page

Each role is an entry stamp, with dates in and out and no place on it. Pick a stamp and the facing page reads its notes.

04

The annotated résumé

The plainest option: the roles as written, on a sheet, with margin notes in your handwriting, as in the case studies. Point at a note and the words it is about light up. One note links to the IVY case study.

05

The file drawer

Three manila folders in a drawer, with staggered tabs. Pull a tab and that folder comes up open, with a FILED stamp.
