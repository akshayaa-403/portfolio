      About

      ☺︎

      Hello. It's nice to meet you.

      I'm Akshayaa Kashyap, an AI engineer who builds the unglamorous middle of a system — the part between a messy source and a decision someone actually has to make.

      Most of my work begins with data nobody wants to open: a CRM export, a scraped lead list, a noisy microscopy image. I build the pipeline, the model and the interface that turn it into something a person can read, argue with and act on. I care more about whether a number can be traced back to its input than about the number itself, which is why every project here shows its working.

      Outside client work I build things in order to understand them, and then write about them on Substack. If any of it is useful to you, I'd like to hear about it.
